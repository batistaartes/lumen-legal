Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

1. Who we are

Alef Automations ("the App") is a private desktop tool its owner uses to upload videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

2. What data the App uses

  • TikTok basic profile information (such as your open ID, display name and avatar), to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, so the App can upload on your behalf after you log in.
  • Creator settings returned by TikTok (such as allowed privacy levels and maximum video length), to offer only valid posting options.
  • The videos and captions you choose to upload.

3. How it is used

This data is used only to connect your account and upload the videos you approve. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. Videos are sent directly to TikTok. The App has no server and no database of user data.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok, as needed to upload your content.

6. How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the App or they expire. Deleting the App's local files removes them.

7. Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions. You can also ask us to delete any data by contacting us.

8. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.
